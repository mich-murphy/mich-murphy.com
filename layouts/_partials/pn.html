{{- /* Older and Newer, under the colophon: the neighbouring posts named by their source files, older at the left and newer at the right. In a newest-first list, Hugo's Next is the newer post and Prev the older, as with a page's own .Next and .Prev. The newest post has no newer and the oldest no older, with no placeholder: newer keeps to the right on its own. The name is the file under posts/, so a bundle shows its folder too (nixos-impermanence/index.md), in a span of its own that takes the ellipsis, so the arrow always shows. Each link's name starts with the visible file name (WCAG 2.5.3), then a hidden ", older post:" and the title; the arrows are hidden from screen readers. Styles are in post.css */}} {{- $posts := (partialCached "posts.html" .).ByDate.Reverse }} {{- $older := $posts.Prev . }} {{- $newer := $posts.Next . }} {{- if or $older $newer }}
